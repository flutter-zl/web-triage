### #184683 - [web] LateInitializationError in CkSurface when webglcontextlost fires during acquireCanvas

- **Action**: triage
- **Priority**: P2
- **Labels to add**: triaged-web, has reproducible steps
- **Labels to remove**: none
- **Title cleanup**: none needed
- **Comment**: none needed
- **Reasoning**: Real CanvasKit engine race condition with Sentry-confirmed production impact and a solid root cause analysis; `team-web` is the right owner.
- **Root cause**: In `canvas_provider.dart`, `acquireCanvas()` registers a `webglcontextlost` listener on the canvas before returning it. `CkSurface` then assigns the return value to `_canvas`, a `late` field. If the browser fires `webglcontextlost` synchronously during canvas creation, `onContextLost()` is called while `_canvas` is still uninitialized, causing the `LateInitializationError`. The fix is to either initialize `_canvas` to a sentinel before calling `acquireCanvas`, or defer the listener registration until after `_canvas` is assigned.

**Note:** `has reproducible steps` is borderline since there are no manual repro steps; you may skip that label and add only `triaged-web`.
